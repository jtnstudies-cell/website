PROFESSIONAL INTRODUCTION

I am Dr. Jithin Paralthodi Abdulhudha, a dental surgeon with more than a decade of clinical experience across India and the UAE. My clinical interests include general dentistry, oral surgery, restorative dentistry and implantology.

My approach is grounded in patient-centred communication, careful diagnosis, evidence-informed treatment planning and ongoing professional development.

My professional aim is to make dental care understandable, support informed patient decisions and contribute to high standards of clinical practice.
